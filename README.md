# Henry Yuan 袁伟恒

**Hyperrealistic Oil Painter** · Founder of Magical Okey Gallery · MO Meta Zoo

Based between Chengdu, China and Dubai, UAE.

## About

Henry Yuan is a hyperrealistic oil painter known for monumental wildlife portraits and cosmic nebula canvases — the *Safari Canvas Collection* / *MO Meta Zoo*. Working in hand-mixed oils on canvas, he paints animals with near-photographic precision: lions, tigers, snow leopards, pandas, eagles and owls, alongside deep-space nebula works.

He is also the creator of **Table Art** — live edible plate painting performed at the table, where fruit sauces become paint and fine porcelain becomes canvas.

## Exhibitions & Recognition

- **2025** — Solo exhibition *Animism*

- **2024** — Solo exhibition *LAND CRUISER*, Dubai Hills Mall
- Signed artist of **7 ART GALLERY**
- Featured on the Times Square billboard (New York), the Chengdu Twin Towers screen, and Beijing Metro Line 10

## Collectors

Works are held by private collectors including the founder of NAFFCO (collector of *Dazzling in the Dark*), UFC athlete Jingliang Li, a Czech international footballer, and the son of Marcello Lippi.

## 中文简介

袁伟恒（Henry Yuan），超写实油画家，Magical Okey Gallery 创始人，MO Meta Zoo 主理人。代表系列为 Safari Canvas Collection：以巨幅布面油画描绘狮、虎、雪豹、大熊猫等野生动物与星云题材。同时首创 Table Art 盘绘——在餐桌现场以果酱为颜料、在瓷盘上作画的高端餐饮艺术体验。

履历：2024 连续两届 DIFC Art Nights（第 17–18 届）——第 17 届入围，第 18 届开幕展参展并接受迪拜电视台采访；2024 迪拜 Hills Mall 个展《LAND CRUISER》；2025 个展《Animism》；7 ART GALLERY 签约艺术家。作品曾登上纽约时代广场大屏、成都双子塔大屏与北京地铁 10 号线。
